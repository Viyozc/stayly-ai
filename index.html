    FAQ

    Is the audit really free?

    Yes. The on-page audit runs entirely in your browser and costs nothing. No account needed.

    Do I need to give you my Airbnb login?

    Never. You fill in your listing details manually. Stayly never touches your Airbnb account.

    We weight title keyword density & character limit, description depth, photo count, amenity completeness, pricing vs your area, and trust signals (reviews, rating, Instant Book) — the same factors that drive search placement and conversion.

    Will this guarantee I rank #1?

    No tool can. But most listings leave easy points on the table, and Stayly shows you exactly where — so you stop guessing.

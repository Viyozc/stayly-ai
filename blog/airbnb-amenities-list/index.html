Airbnb Amenities List: 20 Guests Actually Filter For

Updated 2026-07-22 · Stayly team

Amenities are a filter, not a footnote

Guests search "wifi", "washer", "pet friendly", "workspace". Every amenity you actually have but left unchecked is a filtered-out search. A free listing audit scores amenities completeness against what guests filter for.

The 20 guests filter for most

Wifi, dedicated workspace, washer, dryer, kitchen, air conditioning, heating, self check-in, free parking, EV charger, pool, hot tub, gym, elevator, pets allowed, crib, crib, breakfast, security cameras (disclosed), smart lock, and a well-stocked essentials kit. Tick every one you offer.

Highlight standouts in the description

Fast wifi and self check-in are decision-makers for remote workers and late arrivals. Name them in the description, not just the amenities list — guests skim, they don't scroll the whole grid.

Don't fake amenities

Listing an amenity you don't have triggers disputes and reviews that sink ranking. Accuracy protects your score more than a longer list.
